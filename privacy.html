Privacy Policy.

How information is handled on the Xerauq website.

VERSION 1.0 · NOVEMBER 24, 2026

01 / Scope

This notice covers xerauq.github.io and information received by Xerauq through website-related support and communications. Individual software products may have separate privacy notices. In particular, see Ledgerlyst for its product-specific information. This notice does not describe a future advertising or AI integration as if it were already operating.

02 / Website hosting

This is a static website hosted by GitHub Pages. GitHub records visitor IP addresses for security and may process technical information as described in its Privacy Statement. The website itself has no registration form, analytics script or advertising SDK.

03 / Cookies and local storage

The website’s own code does not set tracking cookies or use browser storage to identify visitors. The logo introduction and software search run locally in your browser. External services may use cookies or similar technologies under their own policies.

04 / Ko-fi and payment information

Support is optional. The Ko-fi tip panel is loaded only when you choose to open it; doing so connects your browser to Ko-fi. Ko-fi and its payment providers process contributions and memberships. Xerauq may receive supporter information such as your display name, email, payment amount, membership status and any message you choose to provide. Payment card or bank credentials are not requested by this website. Review Ko-fi’s Privacy Policy and Stripe’s Privacy Policy for their practices.

05 / Communications and use of information

Information voluntarily provided through support or payment services may be used to respond to inquiries, administer contributions and membership benefits, address payment issues, maintain records and meet legal obligations. Personal information is not required to browse the software catalog. Do not include passwords, payment credentials or sensitive project files in support messages.

06 / Retention and disclosures

Information received by Xerauq is retained as reasonably necessary for the purposes above, including relevant accounting, dispute and legal requirements. Service providers may retain their own records under their respective policies. Information may be disclosed where required by law or necessary to address a legitimate payment or security issue.

07 / External services and your choices

Links to other websites are optional. Their data handling is governed by their own policies. You can decline to load the tip panel, disable cookies through your browser settings, or manage your account and membership directly with Ko-fi. Subject to applicable law, you may request access, correction or deletion of personal information held by Xerauq.

08 / Children

The website is a general-audience software portfolio, not a service directed specifically at children. Payments and memberships must comply with the relevant service’s age and eligibility requirements.

09 / Changes and contact

For supporter questions, use the Xerauq Ko-fi page and its private messaging option if available to you. For general inquiries, see the Xerauq GitHub profile for available contact information. Do not post sensitive personal information in public comments or GitHub issues. A dedicated public privacy and support email will be added once established.

Material changes to website data practices will be reflected in an updated notice and revision date.
